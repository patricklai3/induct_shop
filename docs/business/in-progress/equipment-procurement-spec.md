# Shop Equipment Procurement Specification

## 1. Overview & Purpose

This document provides the decision framework, equipment inventory target checklist, vendor quote tracking, and delivery logistics for procuring all machinery, diagnostic hardware, and shop tooling for Induct Shop in the **Kitchener-Waterloo area, Ontario**.

In accordance with the [Business Profile & Operating Charter](../established/business-profile.md), procurement is strictly phased:
- **Phase 1 (Active Baseline)**: Machinery and tooling required for Tesla-focused mechanical repair and low-voltage auxiliary service.
- **Phase 2 (Deferred)**: Tire & wheel servicing machinery, deferred to an independent future greenlight project.
- **Phase 3 (Deferred)**: High-voltage (HV) powertrain, battery drop, and 1000V insulated safety tooling, deferred to an independent future greenlight project.

Once Phase 1 equipment is ordered, delivered, and commissioned on the shop floor, the active asset register, serial numbers, user manuals, warranty terms, and service contacts will be promoted to `docs/business/established/equipment-inventory.md`, and this document will be archived in `docs/business/archive/`.
