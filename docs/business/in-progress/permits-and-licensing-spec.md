# Permits, Licensing & Regulatory Compliance Specification

## 1. Overview & Purpose
This document establishes the checklist, application milestones, regulatory requirements, and filing tracker for obtaining all mandatory municipal, state, and environmental permits to operate Induct Shop legally.

Once all permits and licenses are issued and active, official permit numbers, renewal schedules, physical display requirements, and environmental compliance protocols will be promoted to `docs/business/established/permits-and-compliance.md`, and this document will be archived in `docs/business/archive/`.

---
