# Permits, Licensing & Regulatory Compliance Specification

## 1. Overview & Purpose

This document establishes the decision checklist, regulatory requirements, application milestones, and filing tracker for obtaining all mandatory municipal (City of Kitchener / City of Waterloo / Region of Waterloo), provincial (Ontario), and environmental permits to operate Induct Shop legally in **Ontario, Canada**.

Once all operating permits and licenses are issued and active, official permit numbers, renewal schedules, physical display requirements, and environmental compliance protocols will be promoted to `docs/business/established/permits-and-compliance.md`, and this document will be archived in `docs/business/archive/`.
