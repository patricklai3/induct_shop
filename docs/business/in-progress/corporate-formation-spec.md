# Corporate Formation & Legal Entity Specification

## 1. Overview & Purpose

This document establishes the decision checklist, governance structure, regulatory tax account requirements, and professional advisory evaluation framework for incorporating and establishing the legal operating entity for Induct Shop in the **Kitchener-Waterloo area, Ontario, Canada**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Entity Relationship Clarification**:
> **Induct** is an existing, independent incorporated company that develops, owns, and maintains the software platform. The entity to be formed under this specification is a separate operating business that will run the physical automotive repair shop and license software from Induct.

Once the legal entity is registered, corporate minute books are executed, tax accounts are active, and banking is established, the active corporate records will be promoted to `docs/business/established/corporate-entity.md`, and this document will be archived in `docs/business/archive/`.
