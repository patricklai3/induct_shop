# Shop Site Selection & Facility Specification

## 1. Overview & Purpose

This document provides the decision framework, physical space parameter checklist, and candidate property evaluation matrix for selecting Induct Shop's physical operating facility in the **Kitchener-Waterloo area (Region of Waterloo), Ontario, Canada**.

Once a commercial lease is signed, the established lease terms, square footage, building layout, landlord contacts, and utility profiles will be promoted to `docs/business/established/facility-lease.md`, and this document will be archived in `docs/business/archive/`.
