# Shop Site Selection & Facility Specification

## 1. Overview & Purpose
This document provides the decision framework, physical space parameter checklist, and candidate property evaluation matrix for selecting Induct Shop's physical operating facility.

Once a commercial lease is signed, the established lease terms, square footage, building layout, and landlord contacts will be promoted to `docs/business/established/facility-lease.md`, and this document will be archived in `docs/business/archive/`.

## 3. Zoning, Environmental & Municipal Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Confirm municipal zoning designation permits automotive repair and EV maintenance
- [ ] Verify existing Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) or requirements for change of use
- [ ] Confirm oil/water separator installed and certified for shop floor drainage
- [ ] Verify local fire department regulations for high-voltage EV battery storage and fire lane access
- [ ] Confirm adequacy of concrete slab thickness for heavy automotive lifts (minimum 4-6 inches, 3,000+ PSI)
- [ ] Verify commercial fiber/cable broadband connectivity available at the address
