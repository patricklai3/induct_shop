# Business Profile & Operating Charter

## 1. Executive Summary & Geographic Foundation

Induct Shop is an automotive service venture operating in the **Kitchener-Waterloo area (Region of Waterloo), Ontario, Canada**.

This document serves as the foundational, active single source of truth for the shop's strategic operating parameters, vehicle specialization priorities, service boundaries, corporate relationship model, and phased project roadmap. Live project task execution and milestones are tracked on the [Shop Opening GitHub Project (Project 13)](https://github.com/users/patricklai3/projects/13).

All operational decisions, facility configurations, tooling procurements, and software workflows must align with the core principles established herein.

---

## 2. Vehicle Specialization Strategy

### 2.1 Priority #1: Tesla Electric Vehicles
- The primary market focus and core operational competency of the shop is dedicated to **Tesla vehicles** (Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X).
- Shop workflow automation, diagnostic tooling (Tesla Toolbox 3), technician training, and digital intake procedures are prioritized around the Tesla platform.

### 2.2 Secondary / Capability-Matched Multi-Make Servicing
- The shop remains open to servicing other vehicle makes and models on an opportunistic basis.
- **Rule of Engagement**: Non-Tesla vehicles will be accepted *only* when the shop's procured mechanical tooling, diagnostic equipment, and technician capabilities support the specific service required without additional unapproved capital expenditure.

---

## 3. Phased Service Scope & Operating Boundaries

To ensure safe, lean, and high-quality shop launch operations, service offerings are strictly segregated into active operational phases and deferred independent projects:

### 3.1 Active Baseline Scope: Mechanical Service & Repair Only
At the initial operating stage, the shop performs **mechanical service and repair only**. The active service domain comprises:
- Suspension systems, control arms, ball joints, bushings, links, and dampers.
- Steering systems, tie rods, and mechanical alignments.
- Friction braking systems (pads, rotors, calipers, fluid flushing, electronic parking brake service).
- Cabin air filtration, HEPA filters, wiper mechanisms, and washer fluid systems.
- Mechanical closures (door handles, window regulators, latches, trunk/frunk actuators).
- Cooling system mechanical repairs (radiators, mechanical pumps, coolant hoses, vacuum coolant fill).
- Low-voltage auxiliary electrical systems (12V / 16V auxiliary batteries, LV wiring harnesses).
- Mechanical vehicle inspections, safety checks, and mechanical diagnostic troubleshooting.

### 3.2 Explicit Early-Stage Exclusions
To maintain focus and avoid premature capital, licensing, and safety overhead, the following service categories are **strictly excluded** from the initial operational stage:
1. **High-Voltage (HV) Components**: No servicing, opening, repairing, or dropping of high-voltage battery packs, drive units/inverters, high-voltage junction boxes, onboard chargers, pyrofuses, or HV cabling.
2. **Tire & Wheel Mounting/Balancing**: No on-site tire mounting, dismounting, high-speed road-force balancing, or tire retailing at the earliest stage.

## 4. Future Independent Greenlight Project Roadmap

The excluded capabilities represent valuable growth avenues that must be treated as **independent sub-projects**. Each will be formally greenlighted at a future stage upon dedicated business case review, capital allocation, safety certification, and facility readiness:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        STAGE 1: ACTIVE BASELINE                               |
|        Tesla-Focused Mechanical Service & Low-Voltage Auxiliary Systems        |
+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
                                        |
         +------------------------------+------------------------------+
         |                                                             |
         v                                                             v
+---------------------------------------+     +---------------------------------------+
|  PROJECT PIPELINE A: TIRE & WHEEL     |     |  PROJECT PIPELINE B: HIGH-VOLTAGE EV  |
|  (Deferred Independent Greenlight)    |     |  (Deferred Independent Greenlight)    |
|  - Leverless Tire Changer             |     |  - Class 0 1000V Dielectric Tooling   |
|  - Road-Force Wheel Balancer          |     |  - Battery Pack Drop Scissor Table    |
|  - 3D Optical Wheel Alignment Rack    |     |  - Insulation Resistance / Megohmmeter|
|  - TPMS Programming Systems           |     |  - HV Safety Stanchions & Quarantine  |
|  - Wholesale Tire Distributor Accounts|     |  - Specialized HV Insurance Riders    |
+---------------------------------------+     +---------------------------------------+
```

1. **Project Pipeline A (Tire & Wheel Services)**:
   - *Scope*: In-house tire changing, road-force balancing, tire sales, seasonal tire storage, and full-chassis 3D wheel alignment.
   - *Prerequisites*: Capital procurement for wheel machinery, dedicated shop floor footprint, and commercial tire distributor agreements.
2. **Project Pipeline B (High-Voltage EV Powertrain & Battery Service)**:
   - *Scope*: High-voltage battery pack diagnostics, pack dropping/resealing, module replacement, inverter repair, drive unit servicing, and high-voltage isolation fault tracing.
   - *Prerequisites*: Class 0 1000V insulated tooling, dielectric PPE, battery quarantine bay, specialized high-voltage liability insurance riders, and advanced high-voltage safety certifications.

## 5. Corporate Relationship & Entity Architecture

1. **Software Platform Entity ("Induct")**:
   - Induct is an existing, independently incorporated software company that designs, develops, and maintains the shop management and diagnostic automation software.
2. **Operating Entity (To Be Formed)**:
   - A separate operating entity (Ontario Corporation, Federal Corporation, or Partnership) will be incorporated/formed by the incoming business owners to operate the physical repair shop in Kitchener-Waterloo.
   - The operating entity will license the software platform from Induct under a commercial software licensing agreement.
3. **Governance Mandate**:
   - The formation, equity structure, governance, tax registration, and banking setup for the operating entity are managed through [`docs/business/in-progress/corporate-formation-spec.md`](../in-progress/corporate-formation-spec.md).
